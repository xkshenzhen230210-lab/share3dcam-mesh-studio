SHARE3DCAM Mesh Studio invokes the unmodified FFmpeg and ffprobe executables as separate processes.
FFmpeg project: https://ffmpeg.org/
Official command documentation: https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html
Raw RGB input: https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-formats.html#rawvideo
Vertical flip: https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#vflip
Build distributor: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
Version, source commit, license and build configuration are preserved in README.txt and build-info.txt.
These executables are distributed under the license in LICENSE; they remain replaceable by compatible builds.
